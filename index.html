Mr Bot

Carry a FinnBot’s head, body and arms to the Bot Bay, then build four Bots in the workshop. Mind the hatches and the Tickers.

How to play

  • Move with the arrows or the on-screen pad.
  • Up / down climb ladders when you line up with one.
  • Walk onto a FinnBot part to lift it overhead — one at a time.
  • Carry it to the Bot Bay (top-left) — the Bot builds itself as the parts arrive.
  • Head, Body and Arms delivered = on to the workshop.
  • Workshop: walk the full length of a part to drop it a floor. Stack head, chest, arms and legs on each pad to build four FinnBots. Drop a part on a nasty for a big bonus.
  • Spray $OIL (fire / space / the OIL button) to gum up a Ticker or a workshop nasty for a moment.
  • An open hatch (red edges) or a nasty costs a life. You have 3.
