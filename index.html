Mr Bot

Fetch the three parts and carry each to the larder. Mind the manholes and Ticker.

How to play

  • Move with the arrows or the on-screen pad.
  • Up / down climb ladders when you line up with one.
  • Walk onto a part to carry it — one at a time.
  • Take it to the larder (top-left) to drop it off.
  • Head, Body and Arms delivered = shift fetched.
  • Spray $OIL (fire / space / the OIL button) to freeze a Ticker briefly.
  • Open manholes and the Tickers cost a life. You have 3.
